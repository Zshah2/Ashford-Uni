Ashford College
Student Information System — Database Schema

Paste this into a Google Doc. Each block is one table.
Primary key is listed first. An arrow means a foreign key.


What this database stores
People, departments, majors and minors, the course catalog, class sections, registration, grades, holds, campus rooms, and the academic calendar.


Design rules
The schema is in third normal form. A fact is stored in one place. A department name lives on departments, not on every student. A course name lives on courses, not on every enrollment. A student registers for a section, and the section points at the course.

Student IDs are 7-digit numbers from 1000000 to 1999999.
Faculty IDs are 7-digit numbers from 9000000 to 9999999.

A student has at most one major and at most one minor.
Undergraduate class years are Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, and Senior.
Undergraduate load is Fulltime or Parttime.
Graduate programs are Master's and PhD.

A course can have no prerequisites, one prerequisite, or more than one. Each prerequisite is its own row in course_prereqs.

Counts the schema is built to hold
Undergraduates: 1,200
  300 freshmen, 300 sophomores, 300 juniors, 300 seniors
  200 full-time and 100 part-time in each class year (800 full-time, 400 part-time)
Graduates: 300
  250 Master's and 50 PhD
Faculty: 400
  300 full-time and 100 part-time
Students in total: 1,500


PEOPLE

users
Primary key: user_id
Columns: user_id, first_name, middle_name, last_name, apt_no, street, city, state, zip_code, email, phone_number, gender, dob, user_type
user_type is Student or Faculty. This is the one person record. Students and faculty do not copy the name and address onto their own tables.

students
Primary key: student_id
student_id → users(user_id)
major_id → majors(major_id)
minor_id → minors(minor_id)
Columns: student_id, major_id, minor_id
major_id and minor_id may be empty. An undeclared student has neither.

undergrad_students
Primary key: student_id
student_id → students(student_id)
Columns: student_id, student_type, academic_year_level, enrollment_intensity
student_type is Fulltime or Parttime.
academic_year_level is Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior.

ug_credit_limits
Primary key: student_id
student_id → students(student_id)
Columns: student_id, student_type, year, max_credit, min_credit, total_credit_earned
One row per undergraduate. It stores how many credits that student may take and how many they have earned.

programs
Primary key: program_id
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Columns: program_id, name, level, dept_id
level is UG or GRAD. Graduate program names in use are Master's and PhD.

grad_student_programs
Primary key: (student_id, program_id)
student_id → students(student_id)
program_id → programs(program_id)
Columns: student_id, program_id, year, thesis_year, total_credit_earned
A graduate student is in one program. The pair of student and program is unique.

faculty
Primary key: faculty_id
faculty_id → users(user_id)
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Columns: faculty_id, office_number, rank, faculty_type, dept_id, email, phone_number, hire_date
dept_id is the faculty member's primary department.
faculty_type is full-time or part-time.

faculty_departments
Primary key: (faculty_id, dept_id)
faculty_id → faculty(faculty_id)
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Columns: faculty_id, dept_id, percent_time, date_of_appointment
A faculty member can be appointed to more than one department. percent_time is that appointment's share. This is a separate table so the appointment is not repeated inside faculty.


DEPARTMENTS, MAJORS, AND MINORS

departments
Primary key: dept_id
chair_id → users(user_id)
building_room_id → rooms(room_id)
Columns: dept_id, dept_name, room_number, building_number, building_room_id, chair_id, email, phone_number, dept_assistant
dept_id is a short code such as BIO or COM. The chair is a person in users. The office is a room.

majors
Primary key: major_id
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Unique: major_name
Columns: major_id, major_name, dept_id
A department can offer more than one major. COM offers Computer Science and Management Information Systems. The major name is stored here, not on the student row.

minors
Primary key: minor_id
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Unique: minor_name
Columns: minor_id, minor_name, dept_id

student_departments
Primary key: (student_id, dept_id)
student_id → students(student_id)
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Columns: student_id, dept_id, declaration_role, date_of_declaration
declaration_role is major or minor.
A student can have one major declaration and one minor declaration. Two generated columns, major_student_id and minor_student_id, are unique so a second major or a second minor cannot be inserted.

advisors
Primary key: (faculty_id, student_id)
faculty_id → faculty(faculty_id)
student_id → students(student_id)
Columns: faculty_id, student_id
One faculty member advises many students. One student has one advisor in this table. The advisor is not stored on the student row, because the link is its own fact.


CATALOG

courses
Primary key: course_id
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
Columns: course_id, course_name, description, credits, dept_id, is_active
course_id is the catalog code, such as BIO101.

course_prereqs
Primary key: (course_id, prereq_course_id)
course_id → courses(course_id)
prereq_course_id → courses(course_id)
Columns: course_id, prereq_course_id
Each row is one prerequisite. A course with no rows here has no prerequisites. A course with two rows has two prerequisites.

degree_requirements
Primary key: requirement_id
major_id → majors(major_id)
minor_id → minors(minor_id)
course_id → courses(course_id)
Unique: (major_id, course_id)
Columns: requirement_id, major_id, minor_id, course_id
A required course for a major or a minor. The same course is not listed twice for the same major.

degree_requirement_courses
Primary key: (dept_id, course_id, requirement_kind)
dept_id → departments(dept_id)
course_id → courses(course_id)
Columns: dept_id, course_id, requirement_kind
requirement_kind is major, minor, or both. This is the department-level list of courses that count toward a major or a minor.


SCHEDULE AND REGISTRATION

terms
Primary key: term_id
Unique: code
Columns: term_id, code, name, start_date, end_date, registration_open, registration_start, registration_end
code is the mnemonic name, such as fall2026, winter2027, or spring2027.

semesters
Primary key: semester_id
Columns: semester_id, semester_name, semester_year, start_date, end_date
semester_id is the mnemonic name, such as fall2026. semester_name is the label, such as Fall 2026.

sections
Primary key: section_id
course_id → courses(course_id)
term_id → terms(term_id)
faculty_id → faculty(faculty_id)
Columns: section_id, course_id, term_id, faculty_id, meeting_days, meeting_time, room, capacity
This is one offering of a course in a term. meeting_days is the pattern, such as MWF or TR. meeting_time is the clock time. room is the room code. capacity is the seat limit. The instructor may be empty.

enrollments
Primary key: enrollment_id
Unique: (student_id, section_id)
student_id → students(student_id)
section_id → sections(section_id)
Columns: enrollment_id, student_id, section_id, status, created_at
status is enrolled, waitlisted, or dropped.
A student cannot have two rows for the same section. The grade is not stored here. The grade is on student_course_results, so dropping a section does not erase the course catalog or the grade history.

student_course_results
Primary key: result_id
Unique: (student_id, course_id, term_id)
student_id → students(student_id)
course_id → courses(course_id)
term_id → terms(term_id)
Columns: result_id, student_id, course_id, term_id, letter_grade, grade_points, credits_earned, created_at
One grade per student, per course, per term.

classes
Primary key: crn
Unique: (course_id, section_number, semester_id)
course_id → courses(course_id)
faculty_id → faculty(faculty_id)
lecture_id → lecture_rooms(room_id)
semester_id → semesters(semester_id)
Columns: crn, course_id, section_number, faculty_id, time_slot_id, lecture_id, semester_id, available_seats
crn is the course reference number and matches sections.section_id. lecture_id must be a lecture room, not an office or a lab. time_slot_id points at time_slot. The master schedule used for registration is sections.

time_slot
Primary key: time_slot_id
A time slot is not one day or one clock time by itself. day holds day_id and weekday. period holds period_id, start_time, and end_time. time_slot_day and time_slot_period are the junction tables. Each junction row stores the two foreign keys.

student_history
Primary key: (student_id, crn)
student_id → students(student_id)
crn → classes(crn)
course_id → courses(course_id)
semester_id → semesters(semester_id)
grade → grade_letters(grade)
One row per student per class. The grade is a letter already stored for that class.

faculty_history
Primary key: (faculty_id, crn)
faculty_id → faculty(faculty_id)
crn → classes(crn)
course_id → courses(course_id)
semester_id → semesters(semester_id)
One row per faculty member per class. There is no grade.

academic_calendar
Primary key: calendar_id
term_id → terms(term_id)
Columns: calendar_id, term_id, event_name, start_date, end_date, notes
Examples: classes begin, last day to add, final exams.

holds
Primary key: hold_id
Unique: hold_type
Columns: hold_id, hold_type
The allowed hold names: Bursar, Academic, Registration, Immunization, Financial aid, Disciplinary, Other.

student_holds
Primary key: hold_id
student_id → students(student_id)
Columns: hold_id, student_id, hold_type, note, is_active, created_at, cleared_at
A student can have more than one hold. is_active is 1 while the hold blocks registration. cleared_at is set when the hold is removed. The hold is not deleted, so the history remains.


CAMPUS

buildings
Primary key: building_id
Columns: building_id, building_name, building_use
building_id is a short code. The campus uses NAB, LIB, and SSC.

rooms
Primary key: room_id
Unique: (building_id, room_number)
building_id → buildings(building_id)
Columns: room_id, building_id, room_number, room_type
room_type is lecture, lab, or office.
room_id is the building code plus three digits, such as NAB102.

lecture_rooms
Primary key: room_id
room_id → rooms(room_id)
Columns: room_id, seats_available
Only lecture rooms have a seat count.

lab_rooms
Primary key: room_id
room_id → rooms(room_id)
Columns: room_id, workstations
Only lab rooms have a workstation count.

office_rooms
Primary key: room_id
room_id → rooms(room_id)
Columns: room_id, desk_count
Only offices have a desk count.
A lecture seat count is never stored on an office row. That is why the three room kinds are separate tables under rooms.


ACCOUNTS

auth_users
Primary key: id
Unique: username
Unique: email
Columns: id, username, display_name, email, password_hash, role, person_id, is_active, created_at
role is admin, limited, viewer, or stat.
stat is the Stat-Dept-Member login. That account can see department statistics and anonymous rosters. It cannot change records and it cannot see a student's name on those rosters.
person_id points at the faculty user_id for that login when the account is tied to a person.
password_hash stores the password hash, not the password.

admins
Primary key: admin_id
admin_id → auth_users(id)
Columns: admin_id, security_level
security_level is view_only, limited, or update_enabled.


How the main pieces connect

One user is a student or a faculty member.
A student row and a faculty row use the same id as that user.

One student has zero or one major, and zero or one minor.
One major belongs to one department. One department can have many majors.
One minor belongs to one department.

One student has one advisor. One faculty member advises many students.

One faculty member has one primary department, and can be appointed to other departments with a percent of time.

One course belongs to one department.
One course has many prerequisite rows, or none.
One course is offered as many sections. Each section belongs to one term and one instructor.

One student has many enrollments. Each enrollment is one section.
The same student is not enrolled twice in the same section.
The grade for that course in that term is one row in student_course_results.

One building has many rooms.
A room is exactly one of: lecture room, lab room, or office.
A department office points at one room. A class CRN points at one lecture room.

One term has many sections and many calendar events.
One student can have many holds.


Why this is third normal form

1. Names, addresses, and contact fields are only on users. students and faculty store the id plus the facts that belong only to that role.

2. Department name, email, phone, chair, and office are only on departments. A major stores dept_id, not a second copy of the department name.

3. Course name and credits are only on courses. A section stores course_id. An enrollment stores section_id. A grade stores course_id and term_id.

4. Prerequisites are rows in course_prereqs, so a course is not limited to a single prerequisite column and does not repeat the course name.

5. Faculty appointments are rows in faculty_departments. percent_time stays with that appointment.

6. Lecture seats, lab workstations, and office desks are on the subtype tables, so an office does not carry an empty seat count.

7. Holds are rows in student_holds. Clearing a hold sets cleared_at. It does not rewrite the student.
